Piano Atlas Studio — Privacy Policy

Effective 27 September 2026

In short: Piano Atlas Studio does not collect, send or sell any personal data. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything you do in the app stays on your device. Purchases are handled by Google Play.

This policy explains what the Piano Atlas Studio app for Android ("the app") does with information. The app is published by VibeCoder1337 ("we").

What the app stores, and where

The app keeps the following on your device only, in the app's own storage:

  • your settings (theme, sound, spelling, and the like);
  • your favourites, saved progressions and the songs you write in the Composer;
  • your practice history, streaks and progress;
  • whether you have Pro: a record of your Google Play purchase (its order number and date) or of a trial code you entered and when it ends.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. We cannot see it. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in Android's settings, deletes it.

What the app does not do

  • It does not ask you to create an account or sign in.
  • It does not show ads and does not use an advertising ID.
  • It does not use analytics, crash reporting or any tracking.
  • It does not collect your name, email, contacts, location, photos, files, microphone or camera.
  • It does not send anything to a server of ours; we do not run one.

Purchases (Google Play)

Pro is bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment and your payment details; we never receive them. To know whether you own Pro, the app asks Google Play on your device, and Google Play tells it whether this Google account has bought Pro, with the purchase's order number and date. That answer is kept on your device only. Google's handling of your information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

A MIDI keyboard or digital piano

If you connect a MIDI instrument, the app reads the notes you play so it can light them up and show them on a staff. The notes are used on the device while you play. They are kept only if you choose to record or save something, and then only on your device.

Backups you make

If you use the app's backup feature, it creates a file with your settings, progress and songs, and hands it to Android's share sheet. Where that file goes is your choice (for example, your own cloud drive or an email to yourself). The app sends it nowhere by itself.

If Android's own device backup is turned on, Android may include this app's data in your device backup to your Google account, as it does for other apps. That backup is run by Google under your account; we cannot see it.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, of any age.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever starts handling information differently, this policy will be updated first, and the date at the top will change.
